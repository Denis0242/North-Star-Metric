# KPI Definitions

- **Activation Rate:** Share of users reaching initial value.
- **Conversion Rate:** Share of users completing the target action.
- **D30 Retention:** Share of users retained after 30 days.
- **Churn Rate:** User loss risk signal.
- **Revenue Per User:** Monetization per user.
- **Engagement Score:** Composite user engagement signal.
- **Feature Adoption Rate:** Share of users using a core feature.
